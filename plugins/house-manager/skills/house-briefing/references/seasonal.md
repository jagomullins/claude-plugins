# Seasonal checkpoints

General recommendations, not this house's schedule. Raise one only when the house has that system on record, and say that it is a general interval unless the record holds the household's own cadence.

**Early spring** — HVAC cooling tune-up · irrigation start-up and head check · roof and gutter inspection after winter · exterior caulk and paint check · outdoor faucet and hose bib check after freezing.

**Late spring** — pool opening · pest control exterior treatment · dryer vent cleaning · window screens · deck or fence wash and seal.

**Summer** — filter changes on the shorter cycle while the system runs hard · garage door hardware and sensor check · water heater flush · septic pumping if due · attic ventilation check on the first hot week.

**Early fall** — HVAC heating tune-up before the first cold night · chimney and flue inspection before use · gutter clearing after leaf drop · irrigation blowout and winterization · pool closing.

**Late fall and winter** — exterior faucets shut off and drained · smoke and CO detector batteries · sump pump test before heavy rain or thaw · roof and ice-dam watch · furnace filters monthly while running.

**Any month** — water filters per manufacturer interval · whole-house filter · refrigerator water filter · reverse-osmosis membrane.

Never present one of these as due without checking the maintenance history first. Something serviced six weeks ago is not due because the season turned.
