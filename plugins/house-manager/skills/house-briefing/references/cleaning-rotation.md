# Cleaning rotation

## Every visit

Kitchen counters and sink · bathrooms (toilets, sinks, showers, mirrors) · floors vacuumed and mopped · trash and recycling out · beds made or changed as arranged · visible surfaces dusted · entry tidied.

## Deep-clean rotation

Two or three per visit, whichever is furthest past its interval. Track the last date for each in the record (`maintenance` entries with `system: "Cleaning"`).

| Area | Interval |
| --- | --- |
| Refrigerator inside, including door seals | 6–8 weeks |
| Oven and stovetop, including hood filter | 8–12 weeks |
| Baseboards and door frames | 8–12 weeks |
| Interior windows and sills | 3 months |
| Shower grout and glass descaling | 4–6 weeks |
| Under and behind furniture | 2 months |
| Ceiling fans and light fixtures | 2–3 months |
| Cabinet fronts and handles | 2 months |
| Mattresses and upholstery vacuumed | 3 months |
| Closets and pantry tidied and wiped | 4 months |
| Laundry room, including behind the machines | 4 months |
| Vents and returns dusted | 3 months |
| Garage swept | 4 months |
| Outdoor furniture and entry washed | seasonal |

## Households with pets

Shorten the intervals for upholstery, under-furniture and vents to roughly two-thirds. Note which rooms carry the most shedding and say so in the list — it is the difference between a house that reads as clean and one that does not.

## Supplies

Track what the household actually uses by name. Flag a supply only when the record says it is low or the cleaning person reported it — not on a guessed cadence.
